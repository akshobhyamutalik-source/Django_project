About PuppyWorld

PuppyWorld was founded by passionate dog lovers with one simple mission: to connect healthy, happy puppies with caring, loving families.

We believe that bringing a pet home is a lifelong commitment. That is why every puppy in our care receives regular veterinary checkups, essential vaccinations, a nutritious diet, and plenty of love and socialization.
